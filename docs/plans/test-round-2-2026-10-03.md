# Test round 2 — fixes from David's 2026-10-03 prod pass

**Status: BUILT 2026-10-03** (branch `test-round-2`). Every numbered item below
is one finding from the re-run of the owner's day-to-day flows on prod, with
what changed and how to check it. Item 15 (no confirmation text for an
appointment created by staff) was NOT changed here: the reminders session
(`appt-reminders`, 493cca93) documents that staff-created appointments send
no confirmation by design — it is a product decision for David.

## What changed

### 1. Softphone: slow to connect, dial retries, late "answered"
- A **Call pressed while the browser was still registering restarted the
  registration** (grant + SDK + socket thrown away) — that was the "had to
  dial a couple of times". `reconnect()` now waits for a registration under
  12 s old (`CONNECT_PATIENCE_MS`) and only restarts a stalled one or one
  parked on its backoff timer. The dialers wait 12 s (`SOFTPHONE_WAIT_MS`)
  instead of 8 before the cell flow takes over.
- **Presence raced the first tap**: the server refuses a dial from a tab it
  has not heard from (409). The heartbeat now lands BEFORE the status flips
  to ready, and a 409 for that reason re-announces and dials once more.
- **ICE prefetch** (`prefetchIceCandidates: true` on `new TelnyxRTC`): the
  browser's answer, which the customer's ring waits on, no longer gathers
  STUN candidates per call.
- **"Hello" before the card said so**: the outbound poll ran every 2 s with
  no first tick; now an immediate tick and every 1 s (`OUTBOUND_POLL_MS`).

### 2. Phone Create sheet
- Swipe down closes it (shared `useSheetDrag` with the More sheet).
- Estimate and Agreement tiles are gone from the phone sheet (desktop menu
  unchanged); **Call** (Calls page, keypad open: `/app/calls?keypad=1`) and
  **Message** (inbox, New message picker open: `/app/messages?new=1`) added.
- Calls and Messages pages show a one-line **Voice plan** note with an
  InfoTip and, for managers, a link to the plan — only for companies not on
  Voice (`components/VoicePlanNote.tsx`, `hasPlan(company, "DISPATCH")`).

### 3. Phone back pill
- AppShell keeps a **stack** of in-app pages (pop when returning to the entry
  below the top, push otherwise) instead of one previous path. The pill
  names the page under this one, and a tap `router.push`es exactly there.
  Inside one section the label reads **Back** (the section name read as the
  current page) — unless the page below IS the section's list ("‹ Jobs").

### 4. Atlas token meter on phones
- A pill at the drawer's top-left (phones only) shows "N tokens left" as
  soon as the drawer opens. Unmetered (whitelisted "full") accounts never had
  a meter anywhere — if David's company is one, nothing shows, by design.

### 5. Appointment after Complete
- Create Quote and Create Invoice both stay available until one is used
  (`hasQuote` / `hasInvoice` from the page); each disappears once a quote
  (via the request) or an invoice exists. Create Invoice also leaves the ⋯
  menu once invoiced.

### 7 / 12. Live cards and the bell
- `public/sw.js` (VERSION v6): a push now reaches **every open /app tab** as
  an in-app card at once; the OS notification still shows unless a tab is
  in front. Desktop cards no longer wait for the 20 s poll.
- Cards auto-dismiss after 8 s while looked at and, new, after **45 s
  regardless** (`MAX_LIFE_MS`).
- **Every push is recorded** as a notice row (`notifyUsers` writes
  `AutomationNotice` with `automationId: null`; automations opt out with
  `{ record: false }` since they write their own). The bell feed shows them
  with an icon by link (quote / invoice / payment / call / message …) and
  drops a notice when the live record is already in the feed.
- "a good time to follow up" removed from the quote-viewed push.
- **Quote approved by the client** → push to owners/admins + the rep, so a
  desktop tab gets a card and the bell keeps it (`lib/quote-approval.ts`).

### 8. Notification emails off
- Settings → Business Info → Notifications inbox: **"Email these
  notifications"** switch (`Company.notifyEmailOff`). Off → `companyNotifyAddress`
  returns null for every company alert; push and the bell carry on.

### 9. Send by text, everywhere a document is sent
- `components/SendChoice.tsx` `useSendChoice()`: when the client has BOTH an
  email and a textable phone (business line registered + consent), the send
  button opens a chooser (Email / Text, both ticked); one channel → sends
  without asking. Routes read `{ email, text }` from the body
  (`lib/send-channels.ts`), no body = the old behaviour.
- Wired: invoice Send to Client, quote Send to Client (text UNticked by
  default — see the risk note), agreement Send for Signature, Collect
  Deposit. New texts: `quoteLinkText`, `agreementLinkText` (lib/sms.ts).
- ⚠️ **Carrier risk, David to confirm**: quote texts were turned off on
  2026-09-24 after Telnyx read a texted quote as marketing (campaign
  TELNYX_FAILED). The campaign is ACTIVE now; texting quotes to clients who
  asked for them is transactional, but a complaint could reopen that review.
  To go back to email-only quotes, remove `quoteLinkText` from the quote send
  route (one block).

### 12. Unsent quote / invoice
- Leaving a DRAFT quote or invoice page (in-app link, phone back pill,
  shortcuts) asks "Send this quote first?" — Leave without sending / Stay.
  No native prompt on refresh. The editors (new / edit) ask "Leave without
  saving?" once anything was typed.

## Test recipes (prod, phone unless noted)

1. **Dial right after load.** Open any page, within 2 s open a client and
   tap Call in app. Expect: one tap, the card says Calling…, the console
   shows "registration already in progress, waiting for it" (not a restart).
2. **Answer registration.** Call your mom again. Expect: the card flips to
   the timer within about a second of hearing hello; mute/hold enable then.
3. **Create sheet swipe.** Tap +, drag the sheet down. Expect: it follows
   the finger and closes past a quarter of its height or on a flick.
4. **Create tiles.** Expect 9 tiles: Client, Lead, Request, Appointment,
   Quote, Job, Call, Message, Invoice. No Estimate, no Agreement. Desktop
   Create menu unchanged.
5. **Call tile.** Expect: Calls page with the keypad sheet already open; the
   URL shows /app/calls (no ?keypad). Back does not reopen it.
6. **Message tile.** Expect: Messages with the New message picker open.
7. **Voice note.** On a test company without Voice: Calls and Messages each
   show one dashed line about the Voice plan with an (i); managers see "See
   the plan". On your Voice company: no line.
8. **Back pill.** Walk it: Schedule → a job reads "‹ Schedule". That job →
   its invoice reads "‹ Jobs" and the tap lands on that job. Job → Edit job
   reads "‹ Back". Jobs list → a job reads "‹ Jobs". A deep link (a job
   opened from a push with the app closed) reads "‹ Jobs" and the tap goes
   to the Jobs list. The label always names where the tap goes.
9. **Atlas meter.** Open Atlas on the phone. Expect: a "N tokens left" pill
   at the top-left (metered accounts only).
10. **Appointment.** Complete one, pick Invoice, back out without saving.
    Expect: the appointment page shows both Create Quote (pill) and Create
    Invoice (header). Save an invoice from it → Create Invoice disappears,
    "Invoiced as" shows.
11. **Desktop card speed.** Desktop tab open but not in front; tester texts
    the line. Expect: the card appears in the tab at the same moment as the
    OS notification; it leaves on its own within 45 s; the bell lists it.
12. **Bell after a timed-out card.** Let a quote-viewed card expire. Expect:
    the bell shows "X viewed Quote #N · Opened just now." with a quote icon.
13. **Notification emails off.** Settings → Business Info → switch "Email
    these notifications" off → submit a booking as a client. Expect: push
    arrives, no email to the notifications inbox.
14. **Send chooser.** On an invoice for a client with email + textable
    phone: Send to Client → chooser with both ticked → Send. Expect: "Emailed
    to … · Texted to …". Untick Text → email only. Same on a quote (Text
    starts unticked), an agreement (Send for Signature), Collect Deposit.
15. **Phone-only client.** Quote for a client with a phone and no email:
    button reads Send to Client, sends the text alone.
16. **Unsent guard.** Save a new quote (lands on the draft) → tap the back
    pill or Clients in More. Expect: "Send this quote first?" Stay keeps you
    there; Leave without sending goes. Send it → leaving no longer asks.
17. **Editor guard.** New invoice, type a subject, tap back. Expect: "Leave
    without saving?".
18. **Quote approved.** Approve a quote as the client on /quote/[token].
    Expect: push "X approved Quote #N" + a desktop card if a tab is open; in
    the bell afterwards.
